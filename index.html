Sonia Bolshakova | Computational Biologist. Get in touch

Hi, I'm Sonia!

I’m currently a Computational Associate in the Nehme Lab at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, where I study how genetic variation influences molecular and cellular responses in iPSC-derived models of psychiatric disease.

I hold an M.S. in Bioinformatics from Boston University, as well as dual B.A. degrees in Biology (with a specialization in Behavioral Biology) and Psychology, both from BU.

This portfolio highlights selected computational projects and tools I’ve developed as part of my work as a bioinformatician.
